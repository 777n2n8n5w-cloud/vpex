Good to know

  • Federal rule: sales calls only between 8 AM and 9 PM in the called person's local time.
  • Holiday bans ({{HOL_STATES}}) follow federal legal holidays, including the weekday a holiday is observed on.
  • 3-call limits ({{MAX3_STATES}}): no more than 3 sales calls to the same person in 24 hours.
  • Do Not Call: these hours don't replace the National Do Not Call Registry or your own Do Not Call list.
