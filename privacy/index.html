Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Questions: support@vpexdialer.com

The short version. VPEX is a power dialer and CRM for sales teams. We collect what we need to run your account and keep it secure. The leads and call records your team puts in VPEX belong to your company; we process them only to provide the service.

We don’t sell personal information, don’t share it for advertising, and don’t use advertising cookies. Optional analytics and Google Fonts only run with your permission where the law requires it.

1. Who we are

VPEX (“VPEX”, “we”, “us”) runs vpexdialer.com and the VPEX web app. This policy covers the website, the app, our server code and how we handle support and billing. Contact: support@vpexdialer.com.

2. Your company’s data vs. ours

Customer Data is what a customer’s team puts into VPEX: lead lists (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, notes and the details for your industry, such as quote or property details), call outcomes and history, follow-ups, texts you open from VPEX, files attached to leads, Do Not Call lists, investor records in the Vault, scripts and settings. For Customer Data, the customer (the company that signed up) is the controller or “business”, and VPEX is its processor or “service provider”. We process Customer Data only to provide VPEX to that customer, as described in the Terms of Service.

Account and website data is information about the people who use or visit VPEX: account holders, reps, managers and website visitors. VPEX is the controller of this data, and this policy explains how we handle it.

3. What we collect

When you start a trial or are added to a team

  • Office or company name, industry, your name, mobile number, email address, username and password. Passwords are handled by Google’s Firebase Authentication and stored only in hashed form. When an admin resets a rep’s password, the new one is set through our billing connector and shown once to that admin to pass on; VPEX doesn’t keep it.
  • Your role (admin, manager or rep), whether your login is active, and when you were last active.

When you use the app

  • Customer Data, as described above.
  • If you turn on phone notifications: a notification token for your device, your device type and your time zone, so alerts arrive at the right local time.
  • If you turn on the calendar feed: a secret link. We keep only a one-way hash of its key.
  • App settings you choose, such as your theme and goals.

Billing

  • Stripe processes payments. We receive your office reference, plan, whether payment succeeded, the paid-through date and the billing email. We never receive or store full card numbers.

When you visit the website

  • Like any website host, GitHub (which hosts vpexdialer.com) receives your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages.
  • With your permission (or, in the US, unless you opt out): anonymous visit statistics, meaning a count of steps such as “page seen”, “demo opened” or “trial started”, with the site or link that sent you, the industry demo and phone or computer. These counts contain no name, contact details, IP address, cookie or device ID.
  • With your permission (or, in the US, unless you opt out): campaign attribution, meaning the link that brought you is remembered on your device for 30 days, so a later sign-up is credited to it. When an office signs up, its name is stored with that source.
  • Your cookie choices, with a random ID and the date (see the Cookie Policy).

When you contact us

  • What you send us by email or phone, and our replies.

We don’t collect precise location, biometric data, or audio or transcripts of your calls. Calls are made from your own phone and VPEX doesn’t record them.

4. How we use it

  • To provide VPEX: sign-in, syncing your team’s list, follow-ups, notifications, reports and the calendar feed.
  • To bill you and manage your subscription.
  • To keep VPEX secure and working: preventing abuse, investigating problems and enforcing our Terms.
  • To support you when you ask for help.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how people find and use the website (only with the analytics choices described above).
  • To send service messages about your account, such as trial reminders, billing notices and security alerts. We’ll ask before sending marketing messages where the law requires it, and you can opt out any time.
  • To comply with the law and respond to lawful requests.

We don’t sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We don’t use Customer Data to train AI models or for any purpose of our own beyond providing, securing and supporting the service.

5. Legal bases (EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Contract: running your account and the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: security, preventing fraud and abuse, and improving VPEX, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent: optional analytics, campaign attribution and Google Fonts. You can withdraw consent any time in Cookie settings.
  • Legal obligation: tax, accounting and responding to lawful requests.

7. Who at VPEX can see your data

Each office’s data is walled off from every other office by our database security rules. A small number of VPEX staff have technical access to all data, which is needed to operate the service. They use it only to provide support you ask for, keep VPEX secure, fix problems, or comply with the law, and never to contact your leads or for any purpose of their own.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account and Customer Data: while the office has an account. If an office stops paying, it is paused rather than deleted, so the team can come back or download its data. An office admin can ask us to delete the office and all its data at any time, and we’ll do it within 30 days of confirming the request. Copies in backups, if any, expire on their normal schedule.
  • Phone notification tokens: deleted after 120 days without use, or when you turn notifications off or sign out.
  • “Message your floor” messages: 2 days. Send receipts: 1 day.
  • Notification delivery markers: 30 days. Daily send and rate-limit marks: 14 days.
  • Cookie consent records: 3 years, so we can show what you chose.
  • Anonymous visit counts: kept as statistics. They don’t identify anyone.
  • Billing records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Data cached on your device for offline use is wiped when you sign out.

9. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in runs on Firebase Authentication. Every read and write is checked by database and file-storage rules that limit each login to its own office and role, and turned-off logins lose access right away. Calendar keys are stored only as hashes, files are limited to safe document and photo types, and paused offices become read-only. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we’ll notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability: admins can download all of their office’s data any time (Settings › Data, or “Download my data” on a paused office). You can also ask us for a copy.
  • Correction: you can edit your details in the app, or ask us.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your account or your office’s data.
  • Withdraw consent and opt out: use Cookie settings on any page. We honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal.
  • Notifications and emails: turn phone notifications off in Settings; unsubscribe from any marketing email.

To make a request, email support@vpexdialer.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. We’ll verify that the request comes from you, usually by replying from your account email or confirming with your office admin, and respond within 30 days (45 days where US state law allows). You can use an authorized agent where the law allows. We won’t treat you differently for using your rights. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. If your request concerns Customer Data, such as a lead list, we’ll pass it to the customer that controls that data and help them respond.

11. US state privacy rights

Residents of California and other states with comprehensive privacy laws (including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, Delaware, Iowa, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Tennessee, Minnesota, Maryland, Indiana, Kentucky and Rhode Island) may have the rights to know, access, correct, delete and port their personal information, to opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling, and to appeal our decision. VPEX doesn’t sell or share personal information, doesn’t do targeted advertising, and doesn’t make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects by automated means. In the past 12 months, we collected the categories described in section 3 (identifiers, commercial information, internet activity in the limited form described, and professional information) from the sources and for the purposes described above, and disclosed them only to the service providers in section 6. We don’t knowingly collect sensitive personal information about account holders or website visitors. For Customer Data, we act as the customer’s service provider.

12. International users

VPEX is built for US sales teams, and our providers store data in the United States. If you use VPEX from another country, your information is transferred to the US. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers, such as our providers’ Standard Contractual Clauses. EU, EEA, UK and Swiss residents can also complain to their local data protection authority.

13. If you received a call from a VPEX customer

VPEX doesn’t make calls or choose who gets called. Our customers do, from their own phones, using contact lists they provide. If you want to stop the calls or ask about your information, contact the company that called you and ask to be put on its Do Not Call list. You can also email us with the company’s name and your phone number, and we’ll forward your request to that customer.

14. Children

VPEX is a business tool for adults. It isn’t meant for anyone under 18, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children.

15. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we’ll update the effective date and tell account admins in the app or by email before the changes take effect. Changes that affect optional cookies ask for your choice again.
