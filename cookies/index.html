Cookie Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. Questions: support@vpexdialer.com

The short version. vpexdialer.com doesn’t set HTTP cookies and uses no advertising or cross-site tracking. It stores some information on your device (in your browser’s local storage) so the site and app work. Three optional items, Google Fonts, visit statistics and campaign attribution, only run with your choice.

Your choices

Your current choices appear here when the page has loaded.

Outside the United States, nothing optional runs until you choose. In the US, the optional items are on unless you turn them off, and we treat your browser’s Global Privacy Control signal as a request to turn off statistics and attribution. We ask again after 12 months, or when this policy changes. Your choice applies to vpexdialer.com in this browser. Clearing your browser’s site data resets it.

Third-party sites

When you pay, you’re taken to Stripe’s checkout or billing portal on stripe.com, which uses its own cookies under Stripe’s Privacy Policy. Links to Instagram, Facebook and other sites are governed by those sites’ policies.

Records of your choices

Each time you make a choice, a record goes to our server with a random ID, the policy version, what you chose, how you chose it (for example “Accept all”, “Reject all”, saved settings, withdrawn, or Global Privacy Control), whether your region asks for consent first, and phone or computer. It doesn’t include your IP address or anything else that identifies you. We keep these records for 3 years to show what was chosen. Your consent ID is shown above, so you can refer to it in a request.
