What VPEX doesn't do

  • No call recording: calls run on your agents' own phones.
  • No voicemail drop and no predictive dialing that rings several numbers at once.
  • No business or local-presence caller ID: agents call from their own numbers.
  • It doesn't check the National Do Not Call Registry. It skips your team's own Do Not Call list.
