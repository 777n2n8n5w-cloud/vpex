Pennsylvania's new telemarketing law: what changed

Senate Bill 992, signed July 20, 2026, rewrote Pennsylvania's Telemarketer Registration Act. It took effect October 18, 2026. Here is what it means for a sales floor calling Pennsylvania leads, in plain English.

The changes that hit a sales floor

  • Calls only 9 AM to 7 PMIn the lead's local time. It was 8 AM to 9 PM before.
  • No sales calls on SundaysNew. Saturdays keep the 9 AM to 7 PM window.
  • No sales calls on legal holidaysPennsylvania already banned these, and the new law keeps the ban.
  • Texts count as telephone solicitationsMarketing texts now follow the same rules as calls, hours included. People can opt out by replying STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE.
  • Ringless voicemail counts tooA voicemail dropped without the phone ringing is a telephone solicitation now.
  • Written consent for robocalls and automated textsPrerecorded or artificial-voice calls through an automated dialer need prior express written consent: a written agreement that names the number, says the person agrees to get sales messages, and says that agreeing isn't a condition of buying. E-signatures can count.
  • Business and cell phones are coveredDo Not Call protection reaches business and wireless lines, not only home phones. Telemarketers must get Pennsylvania's Do Not Call list every quarter, or use a service that does.
  • No faked caller IDA telemarketer can't falsify the name or number a call shows.
  • Bigger penalties for older peopleCivil penalties up to $1,000 per violation, or up to $3,000 when the person contacted is 60 or older.

What to change on your floor

  • Check every Pennsylvania lead's local time before dialing: 9 AM to 7 PM, Monday to Saturday.
  • Move Sunday follow-ups for Pennsylvania leads to Monday, and skip legal holidays.
  • Treat follow-up texts like calls: same hours, and honor every opt-out word.
  • If you use voicemail drops or prerecorded messages, get written consent first.
  • Scrub cold lists against the National Do Not Call Registry and Pennsylvania's own list before you upload.
