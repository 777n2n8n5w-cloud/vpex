Can I make sales calls on a holiday?

Federal law doesn’t ban sales calls on holidays: the 8 AM to 9 PM rule applies. But 4 states ban sales calls on federal legal holidays: Alabama, Louisiana, Pennsylvania and Rhode Island. That includes the weekday a holiday is observed on.

Holiday bans, November 2026 to December 2027

No sales calls in Alabama, Louisiana, Pennsylvania and Rhode Island on these days, in the lead's local time. Every other state: normal hours.

Days to check yourself

  • Day after Thanksgiving (Fri, Nov 27, 2026). It isn't a federal holiday, so VPEX treats it as a normal day. Some compliance alerts list Pennsylvania for it, and Louisiana when the governor proclaims Acadian Day. If you want to be careful, skip leads in those states that day.
  • State-only holidays. Some of these states have their own legal holidays beyond the federal list (Pennsylvania and Louisiana, for example). VPEX checks federal holidays only, so check those with your counsel.
  • Utah and Nebraska also limit holiday calls, but only for autodialed or prerecorded calls. Reps who dial by hand aren't covered by those rules.
  • Everywhere else: holidays follow the normal federal rule, 8 AM to 9 PM in the lead's local time, unless the state has stricter hours. See every state's hours.

What to do on your floor

  • The day before a holiday, call your Alabama, Louisiana, Pennsylvania and Rhode Island leads while you still can.
  • Move follow-ups that land on the holiday to the next business day for leads in those states.
  • A holiday observed on a Friday or Monday is banned that day too (for example when Christmas falls on a Saturday).
  • Scrub cold lists against the National Do Not Call Registry before you upload. Holidays don't change that.

What VPEX does on holidays

  • Every lead shows their local time, and leads in Alabama, Louisiana, Pennsylvania and Rhode Island show "holiday, no sales calls" on the day.
  • The day before, the Dial screen shows how many of those leads are waiting and opens a list of the ones you can call now.
  • Tapping Call on a holiday-ban lead asks first. Follow-ups booked on the holiday get a warning and a one-tap move to the next allowed time.
  • VPEX skips your team's own Do Not Call list. It doesn't check the National Do Not Call Registry, so scrub cold lists first.
